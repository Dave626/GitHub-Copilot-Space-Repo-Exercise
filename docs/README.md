# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

This folder contains OctoAcme's project management guidance, from validating an initiative through delivery, release, and continuous improvement. The documents are intended to provide a shared, practical reference for project teams and stakeholders.

## Project management process at a glance

OctoAcme begins by validating the business need, desired outcomes, stakeholders, initial risks, and resources, then makes a go/no-go decision before planning. Approved work is shaped into prioritized, estimated increments with clear acceptance criteria, ownership, dependencies, milestones, and a Definition of Done. During execution, teams track work on a shared project board, coordinate through regular standups and delivery or stakeholder updates, review progress in demos, and escalate blockers through the agreed path.

Quality is part of delivery: teams use appropriate unit, integration, and smoke tests, automated CI checks and security scanning, code review, and manual QA where needed. Releases require readiness checks, a documented rollback or mitigation plan, deployment verification, and stakeholder communication. After sprints, releases, milestones, or incidents, teams capture learnings in retrospectives and track a small set of improvement actions with owners and due dates. Shared artifacts—including the project one-pager, backlog, risk register, project board, and status or release documents—help maintain alignment throughout the lifecycle.
